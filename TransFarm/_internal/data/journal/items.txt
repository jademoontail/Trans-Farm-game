# Trans-Farm Journal — Items
# Format: [Entry Name] followed by the page text.

[Personal Massager]
A comfort item that gives +10 Happiness.

For Male Farmhands it also provides a +10% Man Milk bonus.

[Brown Collar]
A basic collar that gives +10 Happiness.

Only one collar can be equipped at a time because all collars use the same equipment slot.

A better collar that gives +25 Happiness.

Only one collar can be equipped at a time.

[Pink Collar]
A premium collar that gives +50 Happiness.

Only one collar can be equipped at a time.
