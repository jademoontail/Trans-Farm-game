[A call from Beyond]
A mysterious transmission reaches Trans-Farm from Queen Xilarxia, ruler of the Bimbosian Empire.

Bimbosians hail from the planet Bimbo. Their people were once bigender, but a gene-altering program intended to make the females more "Hot" ultimately transformed their surviving males into females. For the past ten years they have been unable to enjoy the delicacy known as Man Milk.

After discovering that Trans-Farm produces a steady supply, Queen Xilarxia proposes a trade agreement. In return, she offers access to Bimbosian transformation genetics, opening the Bimbosian form to Volunteers once she joins the farm as a Manager.

Bimbosian Farmhands produce Labor and Alien Milk. Alien Milk can be made into Alien Cheese through Cheesemaking.
