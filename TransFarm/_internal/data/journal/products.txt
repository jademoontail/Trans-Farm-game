# Trans-Farm Journal — Products
# Edit the text beneath any [Product Name] to add its final Journal description.

[Cow Milk]
Milk freshly gathered from your cow farmhands is a cut above anything you’d find from an ordinary cow. Naturally sweeter, noticeably creamier, and packed with even more nutrition, its rich flavor makes it a valuable farm product whether enjoyed fresh or saved for further processing. Cow farmhands can be milked using the classic hands-and-bucket method for a traditional approach, while specially designed breast pumps offer a more automated alternative. Whichever method you choose, the equipment and process are designed around keeping the farmhand comfortable while collecting every last drop of this exceptionally rich, wholesome milk.

[Goat Milk]
Milk gathered from your goat farmhands shares the exceptional richness and nutrition found in cow farmhand milk, but with a flavor all its own. Ordinary goat milk is known for its creamy texture, gentle sweetness, and distinctive earthy, slightly tangy character, but milk from a well-cared-for goat farmhand takes those qualities in a much smoother direction. It is richer and sweeter, with the sharper “goaty” notes mellowed into a pleasant, subtle earthiness that complements its naturally creamy taste rather than overpowering it. More nutritious than milk from an ordinary goat, it can be collected through traditional hand milking or with specially designed breast pumps that prioritize the farmhand’s comfort throughout the process. The result is a distinctive farm product with plenty of character, without the stronger aftertaste sometimes associated with ordinary goat milk.

[Sheep Milk]
Milk gathered from your sheep farmhands is exceptionally rich, creamy, and satisfying, building on the qualities that already make ordinary sheep’s milk stand out. Naturally, sheep milk tends to be thicker and richer than cow or goat milk, with a mild sweetness and a full, almost buttery character thanks to its higher concentration of milk solids. Sheep farmhand milk enhances those qualities even further, becoming smoother, sweeter, creamier, and more nutritious while retaining its distinctive richness. Its flavor is pleasantly mild rather than overpowering, with a luxurious body that makes even a simple glass feel substantial. It can be gathered traditionally by hand and bucket or collected using specially designed breast pumps, with either method intended to keep the farmhand comfortable throughout milking. The result is a particularly hearty milk that's delicious fresh and practically begs to be turned into something equally rich.

[Dragon Milk]
Milk gathered from your dragon farmhands is every bit as rich, creamy, and nutritious as the other exceptional milks produced at Trans-Farm, but its draconic origins give it a character that is impossible to mistake. Beneath its smooth sweetness lies a curious warmth and a slight boozy flavor, lending each mouthful a pleasant kick without overwhelming the milk’s naturally rich taste. Dragon Milk is also notoriously moreish, possessing an addictive quality that leaves many wanting another drink—preferably straight from the source. Then again, the same has been said about nearly every variety of milk harvested at Trans-Farm, so perhaps the dragons aren’t entirely to blame. Whether gathered by traditional hand milking or with specially designed breast pumps intended for the farmhand’s comfort, Dragon Milk is a uniquely indulgent harvest whose unusual warmth and flavor make it stand apart from anything produced by an ordinary farm animal.

[Demon Milk]
Milk gathered from your demon farmhands carries much of the same decadent character that makes Dragon Milk so distinctive, with a rich, creamy sweetness, exceptional nutritional value, and a subtle boozy warmth hiding beneath the surface. Demon Milk distinguishes itself with what comes afterward: a lingering caramel-like aftertaste that gradually develops across the palate, leaving behind notes of toasted sugar and buttery sweetness long after the first sip. Like Dragon Milk—and, admittedly, most of the milk harvested at Trans-Farm—it has a remarkably moreish, almost addictive quality that can leave drinkers craving another taste, preferably fresh and straight from the source. Whether gathered through traditional hand milking or specially designed breast pumps intended to keep the farmhand comfortable, Demon Milk is an indulgent harvest with a dangerously tempting combination of creamy richness, gentle warmth, and that unmistakable caramel finish.

[Human Milk]
While most farms concern themselves with milk from livestock, Trans-Farm has developed its own methods for sustainably harvesting breast milk from its human farmhands. Human Milk has a noticeably different character from ordinary dairy: naturally sweet and relatively mild, with a creamy flavor that can carry subtle vanilla-like or lightly sugary notes rather than the heavier taste associated with cow or sheep milk. The unusually productive farmhands of Trans-Farm take those qualities even further, producing milk that is richer, creamier, sweeter, and exceptionally nutritious. Like the farm’s other varieties, it has an irresistibly fresh quality that has earned drinking it straight from the source a particularly devoted following. It can also be gathered by hand or with specially designed breast pumps, allowing the farmhand to remain comfortable while this rather unconventional Trans-Farm specialty is collected.

[Alien Milk]
Milk gathered from Bimbosian farmhands resembles Human Milk at first taste, carrying the same naturally sweet, mild, and creamy character, yet somehow manages to improve upon nearly every quality. It is exceptionally smooth, richer and sweeter than its human counterpart, highly nutritious, and possesses the same strangely addictive, moreish quality associated with Dragon and Demon Milk. This fondness for Alien Milk is hardly unusual to the Bimbosians themselves; on their homeworld of Bimbo, sharing one’s own milk is considered an ordinary and openly practiced part of everyday life. Friends and companions may freely offer it to one another, sometimes freshly expressed and sometimes enjoyed straight from the source, even in public, without attracting any particular attention. Trans-Farm’s Bimbosian farmhands bring that tradition with them, making Alien Milk not only one of the farm’s most tempting products, but a small taste of everyday Bimbosian culture.

[Chicken Milk]
One of Trans-Farm’s stranger accomplishments, Chicken Milk has no business tasting as familiar as it does. Despite coming from your chicken farmhands, its primary flavor is remarkably similar to the sweet, rich creaminess of Cow Milk, followed by just a hint of the distinctive qualities found in Goat and Sheep Milk. A mild earthy tang appears beneath the sweetness while a fuller, almost buttery richness gives it extra body, creating an unexpectedly balanced mixture of all three. Like the other milks harvested at Trans-Farm, it is unusually nutritious and pleasantly moreish, making the bizarre fact that it came from a chicken surprisingly easy to overlook after the first taste. Chicken farmhands can be milked by hand or with specially designed breast pumps, ensuring their comfort while collecting one of the farm’s most improbable products.

[Dog Milk]
Milk gathered from your dog farmhands is surprisingly pleasant, sharing much of the smooth, creamy character found in Trans-Farm’s other animal milks while retaining a flavor all its own. Naturally rich and nourishing, the farmhand variety is sweeter and milder than one might expect, with a soft dairy flavor, a full creamy body, and just a faint savory undertone that keeps its sweetness from becoming overwhelming. Careful breeding, excellent nutrition, and Trans-Farm’s unusual influence have made it considerably more palatable and nutritious than ordinary canine milk, placing it comfortably alongside Cow, Goat, and Sheep Milk as something meant to be enjoyed rather than merely regarded as a curiosity. Whether gathered traditionally by hand or with specially designed breast pumps, the process emphasizes the farmhand’s comfort while producing yet another example of how Trans-Farm can turn even the most unexpected source into a delicious harvest.

[Drone Nutritional Fluid]
Harvested from female latex drones, Drone Nutritional Fluid is an extraordinarily rich, high-fat milk unlike anything produced by an ordinary biological animal. Thick, creamy, and satisfyingly filling, the fluid somehow contains a complete balance of the vitamins, nutrients, fats, proteins, and other essentials needed to maintain a healthy life. In fact, a person could theoretically live on Drone Nutritional Fluid indefinitely without needing another source of nourishment—though eating and drinking the same thing forever would probably get boring long before nutrition became a concern. Like the other unusual milks of Trans-Farm, it is carefully harvested from the drones and prized both as a remarkably efficient food and as another example of just how strangely productive the farm can become.

[Honey]
Trans-Farm Honey is produced very differently from the honey of ordinary bees. Rather than being processed through the digestive system, it is secreted directly by the Honey Bee farmhand through highly specialized breasts adapted to function much like the living storage reservoirs of honeypot ants. The result is an exceptionally pure, rich honey with a silky consistency, intense natural sweetness, and a wonderfully concentrated floral flavor. Careful treatment of the Honey Bee farmhands and their unusual biology produces honey of extraordinary consistency and quality, without the harsher notes or impurities sometimes found in lesser varieties. Golden, fragrant, and decadently sweet, Trans-Farm Honey is quite simply some of the highest-quality honey you could ever hope to taste—and another product that could only come from a farm as unusual as this one.

[Goat Cheese]
Made from the exceptionally rich milk of Trans-Farm’s goat farmhands, Goat Cheese takes everything distinctive about their milk and concentrates it into a creamy, flavorful cheese. Its foundation is pleasantly tangy and earthy, much like traditional goat cheese, with a characteristic freshness and gentle sharpness that separates it from cheeses made with cow’s milk. However, the naturally sweeter, smoother Goat Milk produced at Trans-Farm softens the stronger “goaty” notes, resulting in a more approachable cheese with a rich, creamy texture and a clean finish. Depending on how it is prepared, it can range from soft, spreadable, and delicately tart when fresh to firmer and more intensely flavored when aged. Its exceptional milk also gives the finished cheese plenty of nutritional value, making Goat Cheese one of the simplest—and tastiest—ways to turn a fresh farmhand harvest into something even better.

[Sheep Cheese]
Made from the exceptionally rich Sheep Milk produced by Trans-Farm’s sheep farmhands, Sheep Cheese concentrates that milk’s natural sweetness, creamy body, and almost buttery richness into an especially satisfying cheese. Sheep’s milk is already well suited to cheesemaking thanks to its richness, and the enhanced milk of a Trans-Farm farmhand produces an even fuller, smoother result. The flavor begins mild and creamy, with a pleasant sweetness and subtle tang, while aging can develop deeper savory, nutty, and slightly earthy notes without completely losing the distinctive richness of the original milk. Its dense, luxurious character makes even a small portion remarkably satisfying, turning one of Trans-Farm’s heartiest milk varieties into an equally hearty finished product.

[Draconic Cheese]
Made from the rich and distinctive milk of Trans-Farm’s dragon farmhands, Draconic Cheese takes the creamy fullness, subtle boozy warmth, and irresistible character of Dragon Milk and amplifies every part of it. The finished cheese is dense, deeply flavorful, and exceptionally satisfying, with its lingering warmth making it a natural match for spicy additions. Many varieties are made with hot peppers and other bold ingredients, adding anything from a pleasant tingle to a fiery kick that complements the cheese’s natural draconic character. Even without these additions, the concentrated richness of Dragon Milk gives the cheese a powerful flavor all its own. Whether eaten fresh, melted into a dish, or aged for an even stronger bite, Draconic Cheese is a hearty specialty with enough heat, richness, and personality to remind you exactly where its milk came from.

[Demonic Cheese]
Made from the decadent milk of Trans-Farm’s demon farmhands, Demonic Cheese shares the bold richness and satisfying fullness of its Draconic counterpart while carrying the unmistakable caramel character of Demon Milk. The cheesemaking process concentrates that subtle caramel aftertaste into a deeper note of toasted sugar and buttery sweetness, creating an unusual sweet-and-savory balance beneath the cheese’s creamy body and gentle boozy warmth. Like Draconic Cheese, it pairs wonderfully with spicy peppers and other strongly flavored ingredients, with the heat contrasting against the lingering caramel finish. Aging only intensifies its character, producing deeper savory flavors while preserving that signature sweetness. Rich, warming, and dangerously moreish, Demonic Cheese turns an already indulgent milk into an even more decadent Trans-Farm specialty.

[Human Milk Cheese]
Made from the naturally sweet and unusually rich Human Milk produced by Trans-Farm’s human farmhands, Human Milk Cheese is a rare specialty that the farm has perfected far beyond the experimental versions occasionally attempted elsewhere. The finished cheese is a delicate ivory to creamy white, with a remarkably smooth, moist texture that falls somewhere between a very soft fresh cheese and a particularly rich ricotta, becoming pleasantly springy when made into firmer varieties. Its flavor retains the gentle sweetness of the original milk, combining mild creaminess with subtle buttery, vanilla-like notes and just enough cultured tang and saltiness to make it unmistakably cheese rather than simply solidified milk. Because the original milk is so mild, different cultures, herbs, seasonings, and aging techniques can dramatically change the final character, making Human Milk Cheese surprisingly versatile. Trans-Farm’s richer farmhand milk also produces fuller, creamier curds than ordinary human milk, turning an unusual cheesemaking curiosity into a genuinely delicious farm specialty.

[Alien Cheese]
Made from the exceptionally smooth and addictive Alien Milk produced by Trans-Farm’s Bimbosian farmhands, Alien Cheese transforms that otherworldly sweetness and richness into something surprisingly familiar. Its flavor resembles a particularly mild cheddar jack: creamy, buttery, and gently savory, with just enough cheddar-like sharpness to give it character without overpowering the subtle sweetness inherited from the original milk. The interior is pale, smooth, and pleasantly supple, making it equally good sliced, melted, or simply eaten on its own. What definitely isn’t familiar is its rind. Mature wheels develop a striking green outer rind that is nearly impossible to miss and makes Alien Cheese instantly recognizable among Trans-Farm’s other varieties. Strange in appearance but remarkably approachable in flavor, it may be one of the easiest introductions to Bimbosian cuisine—though, given the addictive qualities of the milk it comes from, stopping after one piece may prove considerably harder.

[Fowl Cheese]
Made from the wonderfully improbable Chicken Milk produced by Trans-Farm’s chicken farmhands, Fowl Cheese combines familiar dairy flavors into a cheese that is far less strange than its origins suggest. Its taste lands somewhere between cheddar and mozzarella, blending cheddar’s gently savory bite with mozzarella’s mild, creamy freshness. The texture is smooth and pleasantly firm when cool, yet it softens and melts beautifully when heated, developing an appetizing stretch that makes it especially useful for cooking. The subtle richness inherited from Chicken Milk gives it more depth than ordinary mozzarella without becoming as sharp as a mature cheddar, resulting in an exceptionally versatile cheese. Whether sliced, shredded, melted over a hot meal, or eaten straight from the wheel, Fowl Cheese proves once again that at Trans-Farm, asking where the milk came from is sometimes less important than asking for seconds.

[Canine Cheese]
Made from the exceptionally rich Dog Milk produced by Trans-Farm’s canine farmhands, Canine Cheese is a dense, creamy cheese with a surprisingly luxurious character. The naturally high fat and protein content of canine milk produces a substantial curd, giving the finished cheese a smooth, almost buttery texture when young and a pleasantly firm, compact body as it matures. Its flavor is mild but deeply savory, beginning with fresh cream and butter before developing subtle nutty and lightly salty notes, while the faint savory character of Dog Milk becomes richer rather than overpowering as the cheese ages. Trans-Farm’s sweeter, more approachable farmhand milk rounds out any harsher flavors, resulting in something reminiscent of a particularly rich young Gouda crossed with a creamy farmhouse cheese. Strange origins aside, Canine Cheese is hearty, satisfying, and remarkably easy to keep eating once you’ve gotten past the name.

[Drone Nutritional Block]
Created by processing and concentrating the remarkably complete Nutritional Fluid harvested from Trans-Farm’s female latex drones, the Drone Nutritional Block packs an enormous amount of nourishment into a compact, dense package. Despite looking like something engineered primarily for efficiency, it has a surprisingly pleasant flavor resembling a mild cheddar, with a creamy, lightly savory taste and just enough saltiness to keep it interesting. Its firm, dense texture makes a small portion remarkably filling, while retaining the extraordinary nutritional completeness of the fluid from which it is made. Packed with the fats, proteins, vitamins, minerals, and other nutrients needed for a healthy life, a supply of these blocks could theoretically keep someone fed indefinitely. They might not replace the pleasure of a varied meal, but when you need maximum nutrition in minimum space, few foods on Trans-Farm can compete.

[Eggs]
Whether laid by a chicken farmhand or one of Trans-Farm’s considerably larger dragon farmhands, an egg is still an egg—the biggest difference is simply how much of it you get. Chicken eggs are the familiar size and appearance you would expect, while dragon eggs can be dramatically larger and often come decorated in far more fanciful natural colors, patterns, speckles, or unusual shell tones befitting their draconic origin. Crack either one open, however, and you’ll find the same familiar culinary staple inside, ready to be fried, scrambled, boiled, baked, or incorporated into countless recipes. Dragon eggs simply provide considerably more to work with, making a single egg potentially enough for a very substantial meal. Whatever the species responsible, Trans-Farm Eggs are fresh, nutritious, versatile, and proof that sometimes even this farm produces something relatively normal.

[Wool]
Sheared from Trans-Farm’s sheep farmhands, this wool may look familiar at first glance, but its quality quickly sets it apart from anything produced by an ordinary sheep. The fibers are exceptionally soft and pleasant against the skin, lacking much of the coarse, scratchy feeling associated with lesser grades of wool, while somehow also being considerably stronger and more durable. Spun into yarn, it produces fabric that is warm, comfortable, resilient, and remarkably resistant to everyday wear, allowing garments made from it to remain soft even after extensive use. This unusual combination of softness and strength makes Trans-Farm Wool an exceptionally desirable raw material for clothing, blankets, and other textiles—and ensures that anything made from it is likely to stay cozy for a very long time.

[Scales]
Naturally shed by Trans-Farm’s dragon farmhands, Dragon Scales are exceptionally hard pieces of biological armor that remain remarkably strong long after leaving their owner. Rather than allowing such valuable material to go to waste, Trans-Farm collects the shed scales and puts them to use wherever their natural toughness is an advantage. Their distinctive shapes, colors, and patterns make especially attractive pieces for jewelry and decorative crafts, while larger or less ornamental scales can be incorporated into construction materials and protective equipment. Particularly sturdy examples are prized for defensive items, where overlapping scales can provide formidable protection without requiring them to be harvested directly from the dragon. Beautiful, durable, and entirely renewable as the farmhands naturally replace them, Dragon Scales are one of Trans-Farm’s most versatile non-food harvests.

[Feathers]
Naturally shed by Trans-Farm’s chicken farmhands, these feathers are considerably more impressive than anything you would expect from an ordinary chicken. They grow larger, stronger, and far more durable, with sturdy shafts and resilient vanes that allow them to withstand handling and wear without easily bending or breaking. Their most striking feature, however, is their color: chicken farmhands can produce feathers in wonderfully vivid shades and patterns, giving each harvest its own distinctive character. This combination of strength, size, and natural beauty makes Trans-Farm Feathers excellent for decorative crafts, clothing, accessories, and any project that could use a splash of color. Like Dragon Scales, they are naturally renewable, allowing the farm to collect a useful and beautiful material whenever the farmhands shed their old feathers.

[Man Milk]
This distinctive salty-sweet fluid is naturally produced by male farmhands from across the many species and realities represented at Trans-Farm. While its flavor balances a noticeable saltiness with an underlying sweetness, what truly distinguishes the Trans-Farm product is the extraordinary scale at which it can be harvested. Through the farm’s unusual transformations, specialized facilities, and highly productive farmhands, what would ordinarily be available only in tiny quantities can instead be collected by the gallon—eventually adding up to tons of fresh Man Milk every day. Strange even by Trans-Farm standards, its sheer abundance has turned an otherwise uncommon substance into yet another reliably harvestable farm product.

[Sweater]
Made from the exceptionally soft and durable Wool harvested from Trans-Farm’s sheep farmhands, this Sweater turns one of the farm’s finest renewable materials into something wonderfully comfortable. The enhanced wool fibers create a garment that is warm and insulating without the unpleasant scratchiness often associated with ordinary wool, remaining soft against the skin even after extensive wear. Its unusual strength also means the Sweater resists stretching, fraying, and everyday damage far better than a conventional wool garment while still feeling cozy and flexible. Simple, practical, and built to last, it’s a wearable reminder that when your sheep farmhands produce better wool, they produce better sweaters too.

[A Dozen Eggs]
A convenient carton containing twelve fresh Trans-Farm Eggs, carefully gathered, inspected, and packaged for storage, sale, or cooking. Depending on what the farm has available, these may be familiar chicken eggs or the considerably larger and more colorful eggs produced by dragon farmhands, but either way they contain the same versatile, nutritious goodness found in freshly gathered Eggs. Packaging them by the dozen makes transporting and storing the harvest much easier while keeping the shells protected until they reach the kitchen. Fry them, scramble them, boil them, bake with them, or simply keep a carton handy for whenever a recipe calls for something fresh from the farm.

[Cheese]
With so many unusual varieties of Milk being harvested across Trans-Farm, it was inevitable that some of it would find its way into the cheesemaking room. Trans-Farm Cheese is a general farm product crafted from whichever suitable milk varieties are available, giving different batches their own subtle character depending on their source. Cow Milk can provide a familiar creamy richness, Goat and Sheep Milk bring earthy, tangy, and buttery notes, while the farm’s more exotic milks can introduce flavors ranging from draconic warmth and demonic caramel undertones to qualities found nowhere outside Trans-Farm. The milk is cultured, formed into curds, and finished into a satisfying cheese that can be eaten fresh, aged for deeper flavor, or used in cooking. With so many possible milks to work with, no two varieties need to taste exactly alike—and experimenting is half the fun.

[Protein Shake]
A powerful protein-rich drink made using Trans-Farm’s abundant Man Milk combined with a carefully guarded blend of proprietary ingredients. The naturally salty-sweet character of the Man Milk provides the shake’s distinctive base flavor, while the additional ingredients improve its texture, nutritional value, and overall drinkability, producing a smooth and surprisingly satisfying beverage. Packed with protein and formulated to provide plenty of fuel for hard work, exercise, or recovery after a long day around the farm, it offers a practical way to put Trans-Farm’s enormous Man Milk harvest to use. Exactly what else goes into the recipe remains a closely guarded secret—but whatever they’re adding, it certainly seems to work.

[Jewelry]
Crafted from the naturally shed scales of Trans-Farm’s dragon farmhands, Dragon Scale Jewelry turns one of the farm’s toughest materials into something beautiful enough to wear. Individual scales are carefully selected for their natural colors, patterns, shapes, and luster before being polished, shaped, and incorporated into necklaces, bracelets, earrings, rings, brooches, and other decorative pieces. Their extraordinary durability means the finished jewelry is far more resistant to scratches and everyday damage than its delicate appearance might suggest, while no two scales are ever quite identical. From brilliantly colorful statement pieces to darker, more understated designs, every item preserves a little of the dragon it came from. Best of all, because the scales are naturally shed and replaced, these striking pieces can be produced without harming the dragon farmhands that provide them.
