# Male Goat comments on female Goat lactation.
The female Goats get antsy when they're full. Milking time settles them right down.
I had a taste of fresh Goat Milk from one of the girls. Pretty good, actually.
The goats producing milk seem proud of how much they can fill the tanks.
I never thought I'd be comparing Goat Milk batches with the rest of the farm.
